Ever wonder how AI understands that "crane" means a bird in one sentence and a machine in another?

 Old AI read text like a slow reader, one word at a time. "Transformers" read the whole page at once! 

  They use a trick called Attention to link related words together. By looking at all words simultaneously, the AI picks up on clues—like "feathers" or "construction"—to figure out the exact meaning of every word in context.
