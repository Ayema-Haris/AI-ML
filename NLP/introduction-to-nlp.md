# Introduction to Natural Language Processing (NLP)

NLP is the field of AI that teaches machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language.

## 1. What NLP Does

- Text classification (spam detection)  
- Sentiment analysis (positive/negative reviews)  
- Language translation (Google Translate)  
- Chatbots and conversational AI  


## 2. Core Concepts

- **Tokenization**: Splitting text into words or sentences  
- **Stemming & Lemmatization**: Reducing words to their root form  
- **Vectorization / Embeddings**: Converting text into numbers for the model  
- **Language Models**: Predict next words, summarize text, answer questions  

## 3. Example

- Input: "I love AI!"  
- Task: Sentiment analysis  
- Output: Positive


## Conclusion

NLP combines linguistics, statistics, and machine learning to make computers understand human language.  
It is widely used in search engines, assistants, and social media tools.
